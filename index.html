Our Heritage & Compassion

Built on Trust, Precision, and Unwavering Respect

Located at Number 2194, Ranonkel Street in Windhoek, Namibia, Elcon Gravestones House understands that saying goodbye to a loved one is one of life's most profound moments. We dedicate our craft to ensuring that every monument serves as a permanent, dignified beacon of memory.

Using premier-grade granite and marble sourced for maximum longevity against the elements, our experienced artisans carve inscriptions, motifs, and custom designs that celebrate unique life stories with grace and artistry.

What We Offer

Comprehensive Memorial & Burial Services

Every family deserves personalized attention when commemorating a loved one. We provide complete manufacturing, lettering, and burial accessories in Windhoek.

Custom Granite Gravestones

Expertly cut and polished granite headstones designed to withstand harsh weather conditions while retaining a pristine, sophisticated appearance for decades.

  • Multiple color options (Black, Grey, Red, etc.)
  • Customized shapes & finishes

Marble Memorial Monuments

Classic white and veined marble headstones offering an exquisite, angelic aesthetic characterized by smooth surfaces and timeless elegance.

  • Fine hand-carved detailing
  • Custom portrait engravings

Memorial & Burial Products

A comprehensive selection of supplementary burial products including vases, kerbsets, plaques, and decorative stone chippings for gravesites.

  • Bronze & ceramic photo plaques
  • Kerbsets & surrounds
